# Everything for a tidier
next-watch list.

ClipBell gives you new video alerts, grouped results and simple ways to filter, organise and protect your topics. You decide how much attention each topic needs.

Alerts

## How would you like to hear about new videos?

Choose alerts per topic and keep your day calm. ClipBell checks once or twice daily; priority topics are checked twice a day.

### Silent by default

New videos collect in your list with a small count on the icon. Silent alerts are included in Free.

### Popup or daily digestPRO

Pro lets you choose popup alerts or a daily digest for each topic, alongside silent mode.

### Quiet hours and sound

Set quiet hours and turn sound on or off to suit your day.

### Priority topics

Priority topics are checked twice a day. Checks are scheduled, so alerts are not immediate.

Smart results

## How does ClipBell keep results useful?

ClipBell groups copies of the same video into one card. Viewing labels and a region tag help you decide which link to open.

### One video, one card

Copies across many sites are grouped together, so your list does not repeat the same video as separate cards.

### Clear viewing labels

Results are sorted into Free to watch, Subscription and Not sure. Subscription results show the streaming platform name.

### Region at a glance

A region tag shows whether a video opens in your region. Availability still depends on the original site.

### Related videos, one tap awayPRO

Open related videos from any result when you want to explore more of the topic.

### Optional on-device matching

On browsers that support it, optional on-device AI improves matching without sending data to ClipBell.

Filters

## How can you get closer to the videos you want?

Pro includes all filters. Narrow results by quality, length and date, then give the sources you trust a better place in your list.

### Video qualityPRO

Choose the quality you want, such as 1080p or higher.

### Video lengthPRO

Use length filters such as trailer, episode or full movie.

### Date and custom rangesPRO

Filter by date or set a custom date range, then choose your sort order.

### Your source preferencesPRO

Block sites you do not want, exclude words such as “reaction” or “shorts”, and put trusted sources first.

Organise

## How do you keep a list you can come back to?

Use seen status, watch later, favourites, history and folders to keep useful videos easy to find.

### Mark as seen

Keep track of the videos you have already watched.

### Watch later and favourites

Save a result for when you have time, or add it to favourites for another visit.

### History

Revisit results you have seen before using your locally stored history.

### Folders

Bring related videos together in folders, whether you follow movies, sports or a few different interests.

Privacy and lock

## How do your topics stay yours?

Your topics, results and history are stored only in your browser on your computer. No account or sign-up is needed.

### PIN lock and auto-lock

Free includes one PIN. ClipBell also offers auto-lock and a quick-lock keyboard shortcut.

### Master PIN and personal topicsPRO

Pro adds a master PIN for personal topics you want to keep to yourself.

### No tracking or advertising

We never see, collect or sell your topics, results or history. There are no ads and no analytics on your topics.

### A separate licence check

Your Pro licence key is checked without sending any topics. Payment handling and support are explained in the Privacy Policy.

Personalise

## How can ClipBell feel more like your browser?

Choose the view that is easiest for you to scan. You can also personalise ClipBell’s toolbar icon and its name.

### Three ways to browse

Switch between list, compact text and grid view.

### Thumbnails on or off

Keep images visible or choose a quieter view without thumbnails.

### Your toolbar icon and name

Personalise the icon and name shown in the toolbar.
