## What changes when you choose Pro?

Free and Pro give you different levels of control. Both follow the same promise: your topics, results and history stay on your computer.

Confirmed Free versus Pro plan differences
| What you get | Free | Pro |

| --- | --- | --- |

| Topics | Up to 3 | Unlimited |

| Silent alerts | Included | Included |

| Standard PIN | One PIN | Included |

| Master PIN and personal topics | Not included | Included |

| Popup alerts | Not included | Included |

| Daily digest alerts | Not included | Included |

| All filters | Pro required | Included |

| Related videos | Pro required | Included |

ClipBell Pro is a licence for extension features. Streaming subscriptions are separate and are not included.
