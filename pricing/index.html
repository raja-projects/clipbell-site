How can you pay?

Planned payment setup: Razorpay for India (cards and UPI) and international cards. Prices in India are shown before 18% GST, which is added at checkout. Buyers outside India pay no GST. Available payment methods and the final amount are shown before payment.

Payment details are handled by the payment provider. Your topics are not sent with a Pro licence check.

Can you cancel or get a refund?

You can cancel any time by emailing support@clipbell.com. A first Pro purchase has a 7-day full-refund window; a lifetime purchase has a 14-day window. Later renewal periods are not refundable except where required by law.

Approved refunds are submitted to the original payment method within 5–7 business days; bank credit may take longer.
