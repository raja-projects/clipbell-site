### What is ClipBell?

ClipBell is a browser extension for Chrome, Edge and Brave that gives you new video alerts for topics you care about. Made in India by Tanishkha, it searches the web once or twice a day and collects new results in one list on your computer.

### Is ClipBell free?

Yes. The Free plan includes up to 3 topics, the newest 20 videos per topic, silent alerts and one PIN. Pro adds unlimited topics, full history, more alert choices and additional features.

### Do I need an account?

No. ClipBell needs no account and no sign-up. A Pro licence key is checked without sending any of your topics.

### Does ClipBell see my topics?

No. Your topics, results and history stay in your browser on your computer. We never see, collect or sell them, and there is no tracking, analytics on your topics or advertising.

### Which browsers are supported?

ClipBell supports Chrome, Edge and Brave on your computer. Phone support is not available yet.
