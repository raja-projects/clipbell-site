ClipBell helps movie fans, sports fans, tech followers, podcast listeners and media professionals get notified of new videos about their chosen topics.

For movie fans

How do you track new movie trailers?

Add a movie or show topic to collect new trailers, interviews and related videos. ClipBell groups repeated copies into one card, so a busy release week is easier to follow.

A few topics to try

New Tamil movie official trailerNew film cast interviewNew show trailer

For celebrity interview alerts, add a focused interview topic. With Pro, narrow results to trailers, choose a quality such as 1080p or higher, and leave out reaction clips.

For sports fans

How do you keep up with cricket and sports?

Follow a team, tournament or match topic to collect highlights and interviews during ClipBell’s daily checks. It is useful for catching up when you have time to watch.

A few topics to try

India cricket highlightsCricket team interviewFootball tournament highlights

Use Pro date filters for a match period. Check the region tag and viewing label before opening a result.

For tech followers

How do you follow new tech launch videos?

Add a product or launch topic to keep new coverage together. Grouped results make it easier to see fresh videos without scrolling through repeated copies.

A few topics to try

Wireless earbuds launchNew smartphone launchLaptop launch interview

Put trusted sources first, save useful videos to favourites and explore related videos with Pro.

For podcast listeners

How do you keep new video episodes together?

Follow a podcast or show topic to collect new video episodes. Watch later and folders help you save longer conversations for a quieter part of your day.

A few topics to try

Science podcast new episodeTechnology podcast interviewEducation podcast new episode

Choose silent alerts, or use a Pro daily digest. Topics are checked once or twice a day, rather than instantly.

For media and PR teams

How do you keep topic-related video coverage in view?

Track a brand, campaign, show or subject from your own browser. Clear topics, folders and source preferences help you review relevant video coverage in one place.

A few topics to try

New product launch interviewFilm launch coverageTechnology event interviews

Use Pro for unlimited topics and custom date ranges. Data stays in the browser on the computer where ClipBell is used; this is not a shared team workspace.
