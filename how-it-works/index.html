A small setup. A simpler habit.

Add a topic.
Let your bell take it from there.

ClipBell quietly collects new videos about the things you follow. Set up a topic, choose your alerts, and come back when you are ready to watch.

01

Install ClipBell in your browser.

Add ClipBell to Chrome, Edge or Brave on your computer. You do not need to create an account.

  1. Use the official extension listing when the install link is available.
  2. Open ClipBell from your browser toolbar.
02

Add a topic you care about.

Start with a clear phrase, such as “new Tamil movie official trailer” or “India cricket highlights”.

  1. Add the movie, team, show, launch or podcast you want to follow.
  2. Free supports up to 3 topics. Pro supports unlimited topics.
03

Choose your kind of alert.

Use a silent icon count, or choose popup or daily digest alerts with Pro. Quiet hours and the sound toggle help you control interruptions.

  1. Choose the alert mode for each topic.
  2. Set source preferences and use Pro filters when you want narrower results.
04

Let ClipBell check, then pick a video.

ClipBell searches the web once or twice a day. Priority topics are checked twice a day, and new videos collect in your list.

  1. Open ClipBell whenever you like and review the viewing labels and region tag.
  2. Tap a result to watch on its original site, or save it to watch later.

A few words go a long way

What makes a good topic?

A specific topic helps ClipBell match the videos you mean. Name the subject and the kind of video you want, then refine your list with filters.

InterestExample topicMake it clearer
MoviesNew Tamil movie official trailerAdd the title and release year when you know them.
InterviewsNew film cast interviewInclude “interview” and the film or show.
CricketIndia cricket highlightsAdd a tournament or match date for a narrower topic.
TechWireless earbuds launchUse the model or product category with “launch”.
PodcastsScience podcast new episodeAdd the show name when you have a particular podcast in mind.

Keep each topic focused. Use separate topics for separate interests. Put trusted sources first, and exclude words such as “reaction” or “shorts” when those clips are not useful to you.

Give it time. ClipBell checks on a daily schedule. If your browser is closed, it catches up when you reopen. It does not promise an alert the moment a video is released.
