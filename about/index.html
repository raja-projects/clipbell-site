A little bell, with a clear purpose

For the videos you’d
rather not miss.

ClipBell exists to make following new videos feel simple. One topic-based list, less repetition and a clear promise to keep your interests on your computer.

Why does ClipBell exist?

Fans miss new releases while the same video appears again and again across different sites. ClipBell brings new videos on a chosen topic into one list and groups copies into one card.

Maybe it is a film you are waiting for, a team you always follow, a favourite podcast or a new tech launch. Keeping up should not mean checking the same subjects throughout the day.

A little less searching.
A little more of what you love.

What is the privacy-first mission?

Your interests belong to you. ClipBell stores your topics, results and history only in your browser on your computer, without an account, tracking, topic analytics or ads.

We never see, collect or sell your topics, results or history. Optional on-device AI, where your browser supports it, improves matching without sending data to us.

Who makes ClipBell?

ClipBell is made in India by Tanishkha, a GST-registered business based in Kanyakumari, Tamil Nadu. ClipBell’s primary domain is clipbell.com, with clipbell.app as an additional domain.

It is a browser extension for Chrome, Edge and Brave on your computer. Free includes up to 3 topics, silent alerts and one PIN. Pro is ₹99/month or $2.99/month, with yearly and lifetime prices to be confirmed.
